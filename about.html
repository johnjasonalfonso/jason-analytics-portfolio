About Me

I am a business and data analytics professional with nearly 17 years of experience across banking, financial services, and telecommunications. With a foundation in Statistics, I combine analytical problem-solving, business intelligence, and technical skills to turn complex data into insights that support business decisions.

Throughout my career, I have worked on banking and lending analytics, credit portfolio performance, enterprise pricing analytics, dashboard development, reporting automation, and statistical analysis. My experience spans UnionDigital Bank, ING Bank, EastWest Bank, Maybank, ANZ, and Telstra.

I use tools such as SAS, SQL, R, Python, Power BI, Qlik Sense, and Amazon QuickSight to investigate business questions, improve analytical processes, and communicate findings clearly. I am also continuing to develop my skills in data science, predictive analytics, and machine learning.

Areas of Expertise

Banking and Lending Analytics

Analyzed lending performance, credit portfolio behavior, delinquency trends, and customer conversion across banking roles. Applied portfolio monitoring, vintage and hazard analysis, performance reporting, and statistical analysis to identify trends, understand portfolio dynamics, and support data-driven business decisions.

Pricing Analytics and Commercial Intelligence

Developed pricing and trading-range dashboards at Telstra to support analysis of enterprise mobile plans. Translated pricing data into business intelligence that helped stakeholders examine pricing ranges, compare commercial positioning, and support pricing-related decisions.

Business Intelligence and Dashboard Development

Developed business intelligence dashboards and reporting solutions across telecommunications, project portfolio management, and banking. Experience includes Power BI dashboards at Telstra, a Project Portfolio Management (PPM) dashboard using Qlik Sense at ANZ, and analytics and reporting solutions supporting business monitoring at UnionDigital Bank.

Reporting Automation and Data Quality

Developed and maintained analytical reporting processes using SAS and SQL, including data preparation, validation checks, automation, and documentation. Focused on improving data reliability, reducing manual work, and making recurring reporting more efficient.

Statistical Analysis and Research

Applied statistical methods to investigate relationships, patterns, and trends in business and research data. My statistical background supports exploratory data analysis, statistical modeling, and visualization, including research involving dengue incidence and climate variables.
