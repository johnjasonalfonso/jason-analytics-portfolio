About Me

Business Analytics | Business Intelligence | Statistical Analysis | Decision Support

Professional Overview

I am an analytics professional with nearly 17 years of experience across banking, financial services, and telecommunications. With a strong foundation in Statistics and extensive hands-on experience in business and data analytics, I specialize in turning complex data into actionable insights that support business decisions.

Throughout my career, I have worked across lending and portfolio performance, customer behavior, pricing analytics, statistical modeling, business intelligence, data quality, and reporting automation. My experience combines analytical problem-solving with an understanding of how insights can be translated into practical business actions.

Most recently, I worked in customer strategy and decision intelligence at UnionDigital Bank, where I analyzed lending performance and conversion trends, developed booking forecasts, explored employer segmentation opportunities, and improved recurring reporting processes. My previous roles further strengthened my experience in credit portfolio analytics, data quality management, statistical pricing analysis, and project portfolio reporting.

My career has included roles at organizations such as ING, UnionDigital Bank, EastWest Bank, Maybank, ANZ, and Telstra. These experiences have given me a broad perspective on applying analytics to business performance, portfolio monitoring, reporting, and decision-making.

Areas of Expertise

Business Intelligence and Dashboard Development

Developed business intelligence dashboards and reporting solutions across telecommunications, project portfolio management, and banking. My experience includes Power BI dashboards at Telstra, a Project Portfolio Management (PPM) dashboard using Qlik Sense at ANZ, and analytics and reporting solutions supporting business monitoring at UnionDigital Bank.

Reporting Automation and Data Quality

Developed and maintained analytical reporting processes using SAS and SQL, including data preparation, validation checks, automation, and documentation. Focused on improving data reliability, reducing manual work, and making recurring reporting more efficient.

Banking and Credit Portfolio Analytics

Analyzed credit portfolios, lending performance, delinquency trends, and portfolio behavior across banking roles. Applied portfolio monitoring, vintage and hazard analysis, and performance reporting to help stakeholders understand portfolio dynamics and support business decisions.

Statistical Analysis and Research

Applied statistical methods to investigate relationships, patterns, and trends in business and research data. My statistical background supports exploratory data analysis, statistical modeling, and visualization, including research involving dengue incidence and climate variables.

Continuous Learning: R and Data Science

My foundation in Statistics has shaped how I approach analytical problems throughout my career. I began learning and applying R during my Master of Statistics studies at the University of the Philippines Diliman, where I explored statistical programming, data analysis, and visualization.

My Approach to Analytics

I believe effective analytics goes beyond producing reports and charts. It involves asking the right questions, selecting appropriate methods, validating results, and translating findings into insights that stakeholders can use.

I am interested in opportunities involving business analytics, customer strategy, statistical analysis, business intelligence, and decision intelligence, particularly where analytical work can help organizations understand performance, improve customer outcomes, and identify opportunities for improvement.

My goal is to build on my professional experience while continuing to strengthen my technical capabilities and contribute to evidence-based business decisions.
